Qizhe Li — Academic Homepage

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer and Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Guangxu Zhu, and a visiting student at the Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data.

My research focuses on intelligent networks and AI-RAN, exploring how machine learning, large language models, and agents can advance wireless network intelligence. My earlier work studied uncertainty quantification, sensitivity analysis, and surrogate-based optimization.

Outside research, I enjoy playing tennis and photography. I am happy to connect, exchange ideas, and collaborate on intelligent networks and AI-driven systems. Get in touch!

QoEReasoner: An Agentic Reasoning Framework for Automated and Explainable QoE Diagnosis in RANs

Q. Li, H. Chen, S. Dai, Z. Li, Z. Hu, X. Li, G. Zhu, Q. Shi

Submitted to IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications (JSAC); arXiv preprint

QoEReasoner is an end-to-end agentic system for explainable RAN diagnosis. Deterministic tools convert KPIs into evidence, a domain knowledge base constrains causal propagation, and a stateful planner coordinates anomaly detection and root localization.

DK-Root: A Joint Data-and-Knowledge-Driven Framework for Root Cause Analysis of QoE Degradations in Mobile Networks

Q. Li, H. Chen, J. Li, S. Chai, X. Li, Y. Hou, X. Shao, F. Li, K. Han, G. Zhu

IEEE Transactions on Networking

DK-Root combines weak operational rules with scarce expert labels for mobile-network QoE diagnosis. Contrastive pretraining mitigates rule noise, conditional diffusion supplies task-faithful augmentation, and expert-guided fine-tuning sharpens the final classifier.
